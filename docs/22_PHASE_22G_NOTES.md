## What it is

The accounting reports on top of the 22A ledger. They **read the ledger only** (posted and reversed journals, exactly like the trial balance) and post nothing, so they work the same on the Tally mirror before cut-over (22I/22J journals are ordinary journals) and on the Pulse ledger after it. Every figure is aggregated in SQL (SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path = ''`, service role only, jsonb); the shaping (trees, subtotals, extra columns, labels, highlights) is pure TypeScript in `lib/accounting/statements/*`, and the page, the Excel and the PDF render the same object (`ReportDoc`), so a figure is identical on screen and in a download.

## Permissions

No new keys. Statements need `accounting.reports.view` (Accountant, Chief Accountant, Auditor, Finance Manager, CEO). The VAT return also opens with `accounting.tax.manage`, which changes the VAT settings. Budgets open with `accounting.budgets.manage`, `accounting.journals.approve` or `accounting.reports.view`; preparing needs `accounting.budgets.manage`. Report settings open with `accounting.reports.view` and change with `accounting.settings.manage`. Cost and payroll staff hold no `accounting.*` key, so they see no P&L. Drill-down links to the general ledger and journals appear only for `accounting.ledger.view` (the CEO reads the statements without them).

**Decision: budgets are approved with `accounting.journals.approve`** (Finance Manager, Chief Accountant), by a different person from the preparer, quoting the sha256 of the content they reviewed (`acc_budget_hash`); enforced in SQL and by a CHECK on the table. `accounting.periods.manage` was the alternative, but the Finance Manager does not hold it and is the natural budget approver. An approved budget's lines cannot change for any role (trigger); reopening (`accounting.budgets.manage`) returns it to draft and makes the reopener its preparer, so it needs a new approval.

## The statements

- **Profit and loss**: Tally's vertical layout. Sales Accounts and Direct Incomes, less Purchase Accounts and Direct Expenses, give the gross profit (the ledger's `affects_gross_profit`); plus Indirect Incomes, less Indirect Expenses, give the net profit; gross and net margins. Columns: one, or by month, branch, department or doctor from the journal lines (`''` = "Not set", with a Total column); or a comparison with the previous period (whole months stay whole months) or the same period last year, with Change and Change %. Groups only or groups and ledgers; ledgers link to the general ledger for the same dates (the ledger is not filtered by dimension). The adjustment period can be left out.
- **Balance sheet**: as at a date (and optionally the same date last year or the previous year end), by group tree: Capital Account (with Reserves & Surplus) as equity, then Loans, Current Liabilities, Suspense, Branch / Divisions; Fixed Assets, Investments, Current Assets, Misc. Expenses (asset). The current fiscal year's profit and earlier years' profit not yet closed are lines in equity until the year-end close (22I). If assets differ from capital and liabilities (only possible with a broken import), the difference is a red line and a warning.
- **Cash flow (indirect)**: net profit; non-cash items added back; working-capital changes; investing; financing; reconciled to the cash and bank ledgers' opening, closing and movement, with the difference shown. A balance-sheet ledger's class is `acc_accounts.cash_flow_class` when set, else the nearest group's, else the Tally group's default (`app.acc_cf_default`: bank, cash and Bank OD are cash; fixed assets and investments investing; capital, reserves and loans financing; provisions and misc. expenses non-cash; everything else working capital); cash and bank control accounts are always cash. The seeded "Accumulated depreciation" (1590) is non-cash, so depreciation is added back. SQL and TypeScript hold the same rules (a database test compares them account by account).
- **VAT return (FTA VAT 201)**: one entity per return, periods monthly or quarterly from the entity's settings (`vat_period_months`, `vat_period_anchor_month`), the last complete period by default. Lines are placed by tax code kind and account: supplies (income ledgers) standard → 1a–1g by the emirate of the line's branch (`acc_entity_branches.emirate`; no branch → `acc_entities.default_emirate`, flagged), zero → 4, exempt → 5, out of scope → listed only; purchases (expense and asset ledgers) standard → 9, reverse charge → 3 and 10, blocked → excluded from 9 and listed; VAT ledgers (control `vat_output` / `vat_input`) give the VAT column of the same boxes. Totals 8, 11, 12–14. Boxes 2, 6 and 7 are zero; the Adjustment column (bad debt relief) is not captured. Checks: VAT booked against amount × rate per box, lines on the default emirate, tax codes on unexpected accounts ("review"), and the lines without a tax code (income, expense and VAT ledgers). Each box drills down to its lines (journal links); Excel carries every supporting line (5,000 at most), the PDF the boxes and checks. It is a working paper: nothing is filed.
- **Ratios**: current and quick ratio, working capital, debtor and creditor days (receivables / all income, payables / all expenses, times the days in the range), gross and net margin, and revenue per patient visit, using the patient-visit count from `fin_flash_inputs` (counts only; no patient data on the page).
- **Exceptions**: journals in the adjustment period or posted after their month was closed; cash and bank control ledgers below zero on a day; ledgers whose balance is opposite to their nature (contra accounts such as accumulated depreciation appear by design); suspense not cleared; manual journals at or above the threshold; lines without the branch or cost centre their ledger asks for (`requires_branch`, `requires_cost_centre`, report-only: posting is unchanged). Each list at most 500 rows.
- **Corporate tax bridge**: accounting profit → add back entertainment at the configured share, fines and penalties, donations, other non-deductible items (ledgers tagged with `ct_treatment` on the chart, Reporting) and any other add-back or deduction typed on the page (not stored) → taxable income → 0% band → rate. Small Business Relief and connected-person payments (TP disclosure above the limit) are notes. Labelled "Working paper, not a tax return".
- **Provisional**: every report says Provisional until every month touching its range is closed or locked for every entity in scope (`app.acc_range_final`); the label is printed at the top of the Excel and PDF too.

## Budgets

`acc_budgets` (entity, fiscal year start, name, status draft / approved, preparer, approver, approved sha256) and `acc_budget_lines` (income or expense ledger, month, optional branch and department, amount ≥ 0, unique per account / month / branch / department). Entry: a twelve-month grid per ledger (whole-entity lines; lines with a branch or department, from a CSV, are kept as they are) or a CSV (`account_code, month, amount[, branch_code, department]`) that replaces every line; problems are reported per line and unknown ledgers by code. **Budget against actual**: per ledger over the chosen months (clamped to the budget's fiscal year; a range outside it means the whole year), optionally one branch or department on both sides; variance, % and a highlight when an expense is more than the threshold over budget (or spent without a budget) or an income more than the threshold under budget; also by month.

## Not verified

- **Not clicked through in a browser** (no Supabase auth stack here): pages, forms, the grid and the downloads were typechecked, linted and unit-tested only; `pnpm build` and the bundle budgets were not run (no chart library is used, so no new heavy bundle).
- The PDF layout was checked only for being a valid PDF, not in a viewer.
- **VAT treatment is a working paper**: box placement follows the tax code on each line; which services carry which code is still OQ-120, and bad debt relief (Box 1 Adjustment), tourist refunds (2) and imports (6, 7) are not captured.
- **The corporate tax bridge** does not cover the interest cap, depreciation differences, losses brought forward or exempt income; the framework question (OQ-125, IFRS or IFRS for SMEs) does not change these reports.
- The cash flow has no special treatment for a year-end close journal inside the range (22I): choose a range inside one fiscal year; retained earnings default to financing.
- Ratio days use all income and all expenses (clinic payables include rent and utilities); revenue per patient uses the finance analytics' visit count, which counts a patient seen by two doctors on one day twice (OQ-93).

## Before first use

1. Apply `deploy/supabase-paste/47_accounting_statements.sql` (after 43).
2. Accounting, Reports, VAT return, VAT settings: monthly or quarterly as on the VAT certificate, the month a quarter starts in, the default emirate; check each branch's emirate under Entities and fiscal years.
3. Chart of accounts, Reporting: tag entertainment, fines, donations and connected-person ledgers, and any ledger whose lines need a branch or cost centre; check the cash flow class of unusual balance-sheet ledgers.
4. Accounting, Setup, Report settings: the budget highlight and the large-journal amount; confirm the tax numbers with the tax adviser.
