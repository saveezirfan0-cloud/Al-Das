## Repair of seeded workspaces (`app.acc_integration_repair_codes`, run for every org by the migration)

- Normal order (paste 44 before 45, 45 before 48): creates 1230 Customer receivables and 4920 Gain or loss on disposal, repoints every asset class's disposal ledger from 4910 (Rental income) to 4920.
- Out of order (45 before 44, 48 before 45): 1220 named "Customer receivables" is renamed "Corporate receivable" and 4910 named "Gain or loss..." is renamed "Rental income", **only when the account has no journal lines**; customers that named 22E's old ledger explicitly move to 1230.
- An account with journal lines is never renamed or recoded: a `NOTICE` names it (for example 1220 holding 22E sales documents, or 4910 holding disposals). Reclassify by journal if wanted. Issued documents keep the receivable they were issued with (their receipts and credit notes must use the same one).

## Left for a person

- **Received cheques not from a customer.** Only a 22E receipt by cheque is linked. A cheque registered with a payer (insurance) whose money also arrives through a 20C remittance, or a cheque whose counter account is a receivable a Unite payment also clears, can still post twice; the register's counter-account choice is the accountant's. Consider refusing a received cheque against 1200/1210/1220.
- **Remittances vs bank entries.** 22D posts a remittance from the remittance batch (Dr bank), independent of the 20C line; a person could also enter a bank entry for the same unmatched line. Match the insurance line in 20C first (then a bank entry is refused).
- **Before the ledger start**, a cheque voucher has no preview from 22D and the register keeps the cheque waiting; the posting preview therefore shows cheque payments as a difference against Tally.
- 1590 "Accumulated depreciation" is no longer used by any class (22H uses 1591 to 1595); keep it for Tally openings or deactivate it.
- Existing lines on 1220 from 22E documents, or on 4910 from disposals, if any workspace has them (the migration's NOTICE lists them): reclassify by journal.
